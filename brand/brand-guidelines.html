03 · Logo useDevio

Clear space and size

Keep a space equal to the height of the letter o free on every side. Nothing else enters it.

Wordmark: at least 72 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print.
Icon: at least 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print.
The wordmark stays in Latin letters in Arabic layouts and keeps its left-to-right order.
On photos, place the logo on a navy panel.

08 · Shape and motionDevio

Shape, light and motion

Shape

Panels with 18 px corners and a 1 px line. Buttons and tags are full pills. Line icons with a 1.6 px stroke. The square dot can mark a bullet or the current item.

Light

Backgrounds stay flat navy. One soft wash of royal and a faint 80 px drafting grid may sit behind the main image of a page, and nowhere else.

Motion

Cinematic but calm: headlines rise word by word, the logo draws itself, product screens tip into place and sentences light up as you scroll.

Ease cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
180 ms taps · 450 ms moves · 1000 ms reveals
Move and fade only · respect reduced motion
