03 · Our product · Tahselly

Unpaid invoices that follow themselves up.

Tahselly takes your invoices from Excel, Zoho Books, QuickBooks or Tally and reminds every client by email and WhatsApp, before and after the due date, in Arabic or English.

  • Receivables aging in 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days, exported to Excel
